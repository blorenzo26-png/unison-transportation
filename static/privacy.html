Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Unison Transportation, LLC (“Unison”, “we”, “us”) provides chauffeured transportation in Nashville, Tennessee. This policy explains what information we collect through unisontransportation.com and how we use it. In short: we use your details to answer your request and run your booking, and we don’t sell them.

What we collect

  • Quote requests. When you use our quote form we receive your name, phone number, email address, occasion, date, pickup time, pickup and destination, passenger count, your message, and any trip estimate you choose to include.
  • Messages. If you call, text, email or message us on social media, we receive what you send us.
  • Site usage. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are viewed and where visitors come from. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, prepare a quote, and plan and carry out your trip.
  • To contact you about your booking, including changes on the day.
  • To keep records we need for accounting, insurance or legal reasons.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so we can improve it.

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t use it for third-party advertising.

Who handles it

We rely on a few service providers to run the website and our email: Vercel (website hosting and analytics) and Hostinger (email). They process information only to provide those services to us. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our guests, drivers or the public.

Privacy on board

We never post identifiable guests on our website or social media (no faces, names or tags) unless you ask us to.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and booking records only as long as we need them to serve you and meet our business, tax and legal obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing Info@unisontransportation.com. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information online.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
